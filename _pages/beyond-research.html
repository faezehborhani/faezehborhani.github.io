Meet Afto 🐈

I recently became a mom to a kitten named Afto! Even in this short time, Afto has become a big part of my everyday life: keeping me company while I work, reminding me to take breaks, and making home feel warmer at the end of a long day.

His name comes from Afto, a pirate cat puppet from the Carnaval show. You can find the original Afto in one of the pictures below!
