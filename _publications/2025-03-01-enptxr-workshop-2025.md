## Abstract

The 3rd Workshop on Emerging Novel Prototyping Techniques for XR (ENPT XR) emphasizes interdisciplinary collaboration in Extended Reality (XR). This paper outlines the workshop’s scope and ambition, and highlights how it has evolved over three years. The growing attendance and submission quality at ENPT XR demonstrate a rising interest in emerging novel XR prototyping techniques.
